Family Activities in Whistler

  • World-class family destination with activities for all ages
  • Dedicated kids' zones and professional instructors
  • Year-round adventures from skiing to biking to cultural experiences
  • Family-friendly accommodations, dining, and entertainment

Why Families Love Whistler

Whistler has earned its reputation as one of North America's premier family destinations. Whether your children are toddlers or teenagers, you'll find activities, lessons, and experiences tailored to their age and interests. The resort's commitment to family-friendly service means you can relax and enjoy quality time together while creating memories that will last a lifetime.

Winter Family Adventures

Skiing & Snowboarding

  • Dedicated kids' learning areas with magic carpets
  • Family zones with gentle terrain
  • Professional instructors trained for children
  • Equipment rentals in kids' sizes
  • Ski school programs starting at age 3

Snow Play

  • Tube park with multiple lanes
  • Ice skating in Olympic Plaza
  • Snowshoeing on family-friendly trails
  • Sleigh rides through snowy forests
  • Snow fort building and sledding

Indoor Activities

  • Squamish Lil'wat Cultural Centre
  • Swimming pools and hot tubs
  • Family movie nights
  • Kids' clubs and daycare options
  • Audain Art Museum family programs

Summer Family Fun

When the snow melts, Whistler transforms into a summer playground. The mountains remain the backdrop, but activities shift to hiking, biking, water sports, and cultural experiences perfect for families.

Summer Activities

Mountain Adventures

  • Hiking: Easy trails with stunning views, suitable for all fitness levels
  • Mountain Biking: Family trails in the renowned bike park
  • Gondola Rides: Scenic journeys to alpine viewpoints
  • Tree Trek: Canopy adventure with courses for different ages
  • Via Ferrata: Guided climbing experience (ages 10+)

Water & Village Activities

  • Swimming: Lakes and beaches perfect for families
  • Paddling: Kayak and paddleboard rentals
  • Playground: Multiple parks throughout the village
  • Skateboard Park: Supervised area for kids and teens
  • Village Festivals: Weekly events with entertainment

Year-Round Experiences

Some of Whistler's best family experiences are available no matter what season you visit:

  • Vallea Lumina: Enchanting evening walk through illuminated forest (ages 5+)
  • Squamish Lil'wat Cultural Centre: Interactive Indigenous cultural experiences
  • Audain Art Museum: Family workshops and scavenger hunts
  • Whistler Village: Car-free pedestrian area perfect for strolling
  • Bear Viewing: Seasonal tours to see black bears in natural habitat (spring/summer)
  • Ziptrek Eco-Tours: Zipline adventures with courses for ages 6 and up

Family-Friendly Services

Whistler understands the unique needs of traveling families and offers comprehensive services to make your trip stress-free:

Services & Amenities

Childcare

  • Licensed daycare facilities
  • Kids' clubs (ages 3-12)
  • Babysitting services
  • Evening programs

Dining

  • Kids' menus at most restaurants
  • High chairs available
  • Early dining specials
  • Family-style restaurants

Accommodations

  • Family suites with kitchens
  • Condos with multiple bedrooms
  • Cribs and rollaway beds
  • Laundry facilities

Planning Tips for Families

  • Book lessons in advance: Ski and bike schools fill up during peak seasons
  • Consider accommodations with kitchens: Save money and accommodate picky eaters
  • Take advantage of kids' programs: Enjoy some adult time while kids have supervised fun
  • Pack layers: Mountain weather changes quickly at any time of year
  • Plan rest days: Balance active days with relaxed pool or village time
  • Ask about family discounts: Many activities offer family packages or kids-free promotions

Seasonal Highlights

Each season offers unique family experiences:

  • Winter: Christmas in the Village, torchlight parades, New Year's Eve fireworks
  • Spring: World Ski & Snowboard Festival, slush cup pond-skimming competition
  • Summer: Canada Day celebrations, free outdoor concerts, farmers markets
  • Fall: Whistler Village Beer Festival (adults), Thanksgiving festivities, autumn colors
