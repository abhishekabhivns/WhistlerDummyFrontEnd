Tourism Whistler

Getting to Whistler

  • 120 km (75 miles) north of Vancouver on the scenic Sea-to-Sky Highway
  • 2-hour drive from downtown Vancouver or Vancouver International Airport
  • Multiple transportation options including drive, shuttle, and private transfer
  • Year-round access with spectacular scenery along the route

Your Journey to Whistler

Getting to Whistler is part of the adventure. The Sea-to-Sky Highway (Highway 99) is considered one of the most scenic drives in the world, winding along the coast past Howe Sound before climbing into the mountains. Whether you choose to drive yourself or take advantage of convenient shuttle services, the journey sets the tone for an unforgettable mountain experience.

Transportation Options

By Car

  • Duration: Approximately 2 hours from Vancouver
  • Route: Highway 99 (Sea-to-Sky Highway)
  • Winter: Snow tires or chains may be required
  • Parking: Multiple parkades and lots in Whistler Village
  • Tip: Leave early to avoid traffic and enjoy scenic stops

By Shuttle or Bus

  • Providers: Multiple daily departures from YVR and Vancouver
  • Cost: $50-100 CAD per person one-way
  • Bookings: Advance reservations recommended
  • Benefits: No driving stress, no parking fees
  • WiFi: Many shuttles offer complimentary internet

From Vancouver International Airport (YVR)

Most visitors arrive via Vancouver International Airport, British Columbia's main air hub serving flights from around the world. From YVR, you have several options:

  • Rental Car: All major rental companies operate at YVR with counters in the arrivals area
  • Shuttle Services: Scheduled shuttles depart regularly from outside the terminal
  • Private Transfer: Luxury vehicles and SUVs available for direct, personalized service
  • Helicopter: For the ultimate arrival experience (30 minutes, weather dependent)

Recommended Stops Along the Way

Take advantage of amazing attractions along the Sea-to-Sky Highway:

Shannon Falls

  • BC's third highest waterfall
  • 335-meter (1,099 ft) cascade
  • Short walk from parking
  • Great photo opportunity

Sea-to-Sky Gondola

  • 10-minute ride to summit lodge
  • Spectacular mountain views
  • Hiking trails and suspension bridge
  • Dining with panoramic vistas

Britannia Mine Museum

  • Historic copper mine
  • Interactive exhibits and tours
  • Underground mine exploration
  • National Historic Site

Driving Tips and Road Conditions

The Sea-to-Sky Highway is a winding mountain road that requires attentive driving, especially in winter conditions. Here are important tips for your journey:

  • Winter Driving: BC law requires winter tires or chains from October 1 to March 31
  • Road Conditions: Check DriveBC.ca for current conditions and webcams
  • Weather: Mountain weather can change quickly; be prepared
  • Speed Limits: Strictly enforced; drive according to conditions
  • Rest Stops: Pull-outs available for photos and breaks
  • Wildlife: Watch for animals, especially at dawn and dusk
  • Fuel: Gas stations available in Squamish and Whistler

Alternative Airports

While Vancouver International Airport (YVR) is the most common arrival point, other options include:

  • Seattle-Tacoma (SEA): 4.5 hour drive; popular for US visitors
  • Abbotsford (YXX): 2.5 hour drive; budget airline option
  • Pemberton Airport (YPS): 30 minutes north; private and charter flights

Border Crossing Information

US visitors should be aware that Whistler is in Canada. Ensure you have:

  • Valid passport or enhanced driver's license
  • Completed customs declaration if flying into Canada
  • Travel insurance (recommended)
  • Understanding of duty-free allowances
