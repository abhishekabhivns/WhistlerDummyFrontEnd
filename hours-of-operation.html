Whistler Hours of Operation

Plan Your Day

Operating hours in Whistler change throughout the year to accommodate seasonal activities and visitor needs. Winter sees extended mountain operating hours, while summer focuses on sightseeing and recreational activities. Always check current conditions and hours before heading out, as weather and special events may cause adjustments.

Mountain Operations

Winter Season (November - April)

  • Lifts Open: 8:00 AM - 3:00 PM (weather permitting)
  • Peak 2 Peak Gondola: 8:30 AM - 2:30 PM
  • Creekside Gondola: 8:00 AM - 3:00 PM
  • Alpine Lifts: May close earlier due to weather
  • Extended Hours: Holiday periods and spring skiing
  • First Tracks: 7:15 AM (select days, ticket required)

Summer Season (May - October)

  • Whistler Village Gondola: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM
  • Peak 2 Peak Gondola: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM
  • Bike Park: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM (July-August)
  • Mountain Top Activities: 10:00 AM - 4:30 PM
  • Hiking Trails: Sunrise to sunset
  • Last Upload: Typically 4:30 PM

Note: Mountain operations are subject to weather conditions and may change without notice. Check the Whistler Blackcomb app or website for real-time updates.

Village Services

Whistler Village maintains year-round services with seasonal variations to meet visitor needs:

Shops & Dining

Retail Shops

  • Regular Hours: 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Peak Season: 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM
  • Grocery Stores: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Ski/Bike Rentals: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM

Restaurants

  • Breakfast: 7:00 AM - 11:00 AM
  • Lunch: 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM
  • Dinner: 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM
  • Bars: Until 1:00 AM - 2:00 AM

Services

  • Visitor Centre: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
  • Guest Relations: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
  • Lost & Found: 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • Medical Clinic: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM

Recreation & Attractions

Whistler's year-round attractions maintain regular schedules with seasonal adjustments:

  • Audain Art Museum: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM (closed Tuesdays)
  • Squamish Lil'wat Cultural Centre: 9:30 AM - 5:00 PM daily
  • Whistler Public Library: Monday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM
  • Meadow Park Sports Centre: 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM (hours vary by facility)
  • Whistler Olympic Plaza: 24 hours (ice skating seasonal)
  • Vallea Lumina: 7:00 PM - 10:00 PM (seasonal, May-October)

Transportation

Getting around Whistler is convenient with multiple transportation options:

Transit & Parking

Free Public Transit

  • Winter (Dec-Apr): 6:00 AM - 3:00 AM
  • Summer (May-Nov): 6:00 AM - 12:00 AM
  • Frequency: 10-20 minutes during peak times
  • Night Service: Extended hours on weekends

Parking Facilities

  • Village Parkades: 24 hours, 7 days a week
  • Day Lots: 6:00 AM - 12:00 AM
  • Free Lots: 24 hours with shuttle service
  • On-Street: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM (paid)

Seasonal Variations

Be aware of these seasonal considerations when planning your visit:

  • Peak Winter (Dec-Mar): Extended hours for most services, highest visitor volume
  • Spring Skiing (Apr-May): Reduced mountain hours as season winds down
  • Summer High Season (Jul-Aug): All attractions open, longest daylight hours
  • Fall Shoulder (Sep-Nov): Some facilities on reduced schedules, many close in October
  • Holiday Periods: Extended hours and special events, book services in advance

Check Before You Go

  • Visit whistler.com for updated attraction hours
