A Sanctuary of Relaxation

Scandinave Spa Whistler offers an authentic Nordic spa experience set in a stunning natural forest environment. Inspired by the ancient Scandinavian tradition of hot and cold bathing, this award-winning wellness retreat provides the perfect escape to unwind, rejuvenate, and restore balance to body and mind.

Located just 10 minutes north of Whistler Village at the base of Cougar Mountain, the spa seamlessly blends architectural beauty with the surrounding coastal rainforest. Whether you're seeking relief after an active day on the mountains or simply looking to decompress, Scandinave Spa delivers a deeply restorative experience.

The Hydrotherapy Experience

At the heart of Scandinave Spa is the therapeutic practice of hydrotherapy - alternating between hot and cold water to stimulate circulation, eliminate toxins, and promote deep relaxation.

The Three-Step Circuit

1. Warm (10-15 minutes)

  • Begin in hot baths (39-40°C)
  • Eucalyptus steam bath
  • Traditional Finnish sauna
  • Wood-burning stove warmth
  • Opens pores and relaxes muscles

2. Cold (10-15 seconds)

  • Plunge into cold baths
  • Nordic waterfall showers
  • Bracing outdoor experience
  • Stimulates circulation
  • Invigorates and energizes

3. Relax (15-20 minutes)

  • Rest in relaxation areas
  • Hammocks under evergreens
  • Cozy solarium spaces
  • Body normalizes temperature
  • Maximum therapeutic benefit

Repeat the cycle 2-3 times for optimal results. Allow 2-3 hours for your complete spa experience.

Spa Facilities

Scandinave Spa features a variety of hot and cold baths, steam rooms, and relaxation areas scattered throughout the serene forest setting:

  • Hot Baths: Multiple outdoor baths heated to 39-40°C with stunning forest views
  • Eucalyptus Steam Bath: Therapeutic steam infused with aromatic eucalyptus
  • Finnish Sauna: Traditional dry heat sauna with wood-burning stove
  • Cold Plunge Pools: Invigorating cold water pools to stimulate circulation
  • Nordic Waterfall: Cascading cold water showers for a refreshing contrast
  • Relaxation Areas: Quiet zones with loungers, hammocks, and fireplaces throughout the property
  • Solarium: Indoor relaxation space with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the forest

Massage Treatments

Enhance your spa experience with professional massage therapy. Certified therapists offer a range of treatments designed to complement the hydrotherapy circuit:

Treatment Options

Massage Types

  • Swedish Massage: Classic relaxation technique (45/60/90 min)
  • Deep Tissue: Intensive pressure for chronic tension (45/60/90 min)
  • Hot Stone: Heated stones melt away stress (60/90 min)
  • Exfoliation: Body scrub followed by moisturizing treatment
  • Couple's Massage: Side-by-side treatments in private room

Booking Information

Visitor Guidelines

To ensure a peaceful experience for all guests, Scandinave Spa maintains important policies:

  • Adults Only: Must be 19 years or older to enter the spa
  • Reservations Required: Book online in advance, especially during peak seasons
  • Silence Policy: Quiet is observed throughout the spa to maintain tranquility
  • Bathing Suits Required: Appropriate swimwear must be worn at all times
  • No Electronics: Cell phones and cameras not permitted in spa areas
  • Towels & Robes Provided: Complimentary towels and robes included with admission
  • Lockers Available: Secure storage for personal belongings

Getting There

Scandinave Spa is located at 8010 Mons Road, approximately 10 minutes north of Whistler Village:
