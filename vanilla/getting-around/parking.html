Parking in Whistler

  • Multiple parkades and day lots throughout the village
  • Free parking available in designated day lots with shuttle service
  • On-street parking in select areas
  • Overnight parking options for visitors staying in the village

Village Parkades

Whistler Village features several multi-level parkades conveniently located near shops, restaurants, and ski lifts. These climate-controlled facilities offer covered parking and are ideal for both day visitors and overnight guests.

Main Parkades

Village Gate Parkade

  • 600+ spaces
  • Direct access to Village Stroll
  • Credit card payment
  • EV charging stations available

Conference Centre Parkade

  • 500+ spaces
  • Adjacent to Whistler Conference Centre
  • Close to Blackcomb base
  • Accessible parking available

Marketplace Parkade

  • 400+ spaces
  • Convenient for shopping and dining
  • Short-term and long-term rates
  • 24/7 access

Day Lots and Free Parking

For budget-conscious visitors, Whistler offers several free parking options in designated day lots. These lots are located a short distance from the village core, with complimentary shuttle service running regularly throughout the day.

Popular free parking locations include:

  • Day Lot 4 & 5: Largest free lots, frequent shuttle service to village
  • Creekside: Free parking near Creekside Gondola
  • Function Junction: Industrial area with free parking and transit access

Parking Rates

Parking rates vary by location, season, and duration. During peak winter and summer seasons, rates are typically higher due to increased demand.

Typical Rates (Subject to Change)

Day Parking (Parkades)

  • First hour: $5
  • 2-4 hours: $3 per hour
  • 4-8 hours: $25 flat rate
  • 8+ hours: $35 flat rate
  • Early bird special: $20 (arrive before 8 AM)

Overnight Parking

  • 24-hour rate: $45
  • Weekly rate: $250
  • Monthly rate: $800
  • Reservations recommended during peak season

Note: Free parking available in designated day lots. Rates shown are for village parkades and subject to change.

Tips for Parking in Whistler

  • Arrive early: Parking fills up quickly during peak times, especially on powder days and holidays
  • Use transit: Consider the free public transit system to avoid parking hassles
  • Book ahead: Reserve overnight parking in advance during busy periods
  • Download apps: Use parking apps for real-time availability and mobile payment
  • Carpool: Share rides to reduce congestion and save on parking costs
  • EV charging: Several parkades offer electric vehicle charging stations

Parking Regulations

Please observe all posted parking signs and time limits. Whistler enforces parking regulations year-round, and violations may result in tickets or towing. Do not park in fire lanes, loading zones, or residential areas without permits.

During winter months, overnight parking restrictions are in effect on many village streets to allow for snow clearing operations.
