Pricing Guide

Lift ticket prices vary by season, date purchased, and advance booking. Generally, tickets purchased online in advance offer significant savings compared to window prices.

Typical Pricing (Subject to Change)

Adult Tickets (19-64)

  • 1-Day: $189-239 (peak vs. value days)
  • 3-Day: $549 ($183/day average)
  • 5-Day: $869 ($174/day average)
  • 7-Day: $1,149 ($164/day average)
  • Online advance purchase recommended

Youth & Seniors

  • Youth (13-18): 25% discount
  • Senior (65+): 25% discount
  • Child (7-12): 40% discount
  • Under 7: Free with adult ticket
  • Kids Ski Free: Special promotions apply

Prices are approximate and change based on demand, season, and booking date. Always check whistlerblackcomb.com for current pricing.

The Epic Pass Family

The Epic Pass opens up a world of skiing beyond Whistler Blackcomb. Choose from several tiers based on your skiing frequency and destination preferences:

  • Epic Pass: Unlimited skiing at all Vail Resorts properties including Whistler Blackcomb, plus partner resorts worldwide
  • Epic Local Pass: Unlimited with holiday restrictions; best for flexible skiers
  • Epic Day Pass: 1-7 days anywhere; choose your adventure days
  • Military Epic Pass: Exclusive discounts for active and retired military

Early season purchase (spring/summer) offers the best pricing. Passes typically go on sale in March with tiered pricing through fall.

Purchasing Tips

  • Buy online: Save time and often money compared to window prices
  • Upload your photo: Skip the ticket office by uploading your photo to your account in advance
  • Mobile tickets: Download the app and access tickets directly from your phone
  • Calculate your days: If skiing 7+ days, consider a season pass for better value
  • Check for deals: Early season promotions, group rates, and packages save money
  • Kids ski free: One child 12 and under skis free with each adult ticket purchase (select dates)
  • Spring skiing: Late season offers best value and excellent conditions

Refunds & Insurance

Lift tickets are generally non-refundable, but you have options for protection:

  • Ticket insurance: Available at purchase to protect against illness, injury, or travel disruption
  • Pass insurance: Epic Coverage protects your season pass investment
  • Weather closures: If lifts don't operate due to weather, refunds or credits may be issued
  • Unused days: Multi-day tickets can sometimes be applied to future purchases (restrictions apply)

Special Programs

Whistler Blackcomb offers specialized programs for various groups:

  • School & Group Programs: Discounted rates for organized groups and school trips
  • Corporate Passes: Business programs for employee benefits and team building
  • Learn to Ski/Ride Packages: Beginner packages including lesson, rentals, and lift access
  • Adaptive Programs: Specialized access and support for adaptive skiers

Getting Started

Ready to hit the slopes? Here's what to do:

  1. Visit whistlerblackcomb.com to view current ticket options and pricing
  2. Create an account or log in to your existing Epic account
  3. Select your ticket or pass option and complete purchase
  4. Upload your photo to skip ticket window lines
  5. Download the Whistler Blackcomb app for mobile ticket access
  6. Arrive at the mountain and scan your pass at any lift
