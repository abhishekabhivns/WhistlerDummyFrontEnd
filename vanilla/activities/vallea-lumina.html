Vallea Lumina

  • An enchanted night walk illuminated by magical projections, lighting and soundscapes
  • Journey through old-growth forests along a 1.5 km trail
  • Family-friendly experience suitable for all ages
  • Open seasonally from late spring through fall

The Experience

Vallea Lumina is an enchanting multimedia experience set in an old-growth forest at the base of Cougar Mountain, just minutes from Whistler Village. This unique night walk tells the story of two travelers who discover a hidden valley filled with wondrous spirits.

As you walk the 1.5 km trail, you'll be immersed in a world of light, video projections, and soundscapes that bring the forest to life. The experience takes approximately 50 minutes and is accessible for all ages and fitness levels.

What to Bring

The experience takes place outdoors rain or shine. We recommend bringing:

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Weather-appropriate clothing (layers recommended)
  • Light rain jacket if weather calls for it

Getting There

Vallea Lumina is located at the base of Cougar Mountain, approximately 10 minutes north of Whistler Village. Free parking is available on-site. Shuttle services from the village are also available during peak season.

Accessibility

The trail is mostly flat and well-maintained, making it accessible for strollers and wheelchairs. However, there are some natural terrain variations. Please contact us in advance if you have specific accessibility requirements.
